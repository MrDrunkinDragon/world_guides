# The Wolf Princess's Claim — World Lore

A player's guide to the Aurelian Empire: its kingdoms, its peoples, and the uneasy peace with Ashmark.

---

## At a Glance

- **Era:** Victorian-era fantasy. Blades, bows, polearms, and magic.
- **Magic:** rare in the general population. Nobles tend to have the strongest.
- **Peoples:** humans, beastkin, elves, dwarves, and halflings.
- **Beastkin:** effectively human, with animal ears and a tail. Hair color always matches the fur. Named by species: wolfkin, bearkin, foxkin, tigerkin, and so on.
- **Calendar:** years count from the Empire's founding. The story begins on Sunday, 1 September 782.

## The Aurelian Empire

A union of kingdoms. Each kingdom keeps its own king and royal house, and all of them answer to the **Emperor**, who is also King of Aurelia.

- The Eastern March is a province held by the Empire itself, not by any kingdom.
- Ashmark is the newest member. It joined in 748.
- Beastkin from every kingdom except Ashmark are ordinary citizens. The prejudice is against Ashmark, not against beastkin.

### Social Classes (highest to lowest)

1. **Royalty** — the Imperial family and the royal houses of the member kingdoms.
2. **Nobles** — usually the strongest magic. Split into upper and lesser ranks.
3. **Merchants** — wealthy, but common-born.
4. **Commoners** — most of the population.
5. **Slaves** — only in Ashmark. Mostly prisoners of war, of every people. The Empire dislikes it and looks away.

---

## The Aldwater

The great river, and the Empire's main road for trade.

- Rises in Ashmark's mountains, spreads into the Eastern March's lakes and wetlands, then runs west through Halden, past Aurelis, to the sea at Maressa.
- It crosses many kingdoms and belongs to none.
- Barges carry Ashmark's ore and metal downriver. The tribute grain travels upriver.
- The river valley is the natural road for an army, in either direction.

## The Kingdoms

### Aurelia — the Heartland (center)

The founding kingdom. The Emperor rules it as its king.

- **Capital:** Aurelis, on the Aldwater. The Empire's chief economic hub and home of the Royal Magical College.
- **People:** every people, from every kingdom.
- **Look and feel:** stone and wrought iron, gaslit streets, grand halls and spires.
- **Fashion:** tailored coats, waistcoats, high collars, corsetry, and full skirts.

### Halden — the Breadbasket (east-central)

Irish shire country of open plains and rolling hills, sitting just behind the Eastern March.

- **People:** humans, halflings, and beastkin: mouse, sheep, rabbit, squirrel, dog, cat, fox, and bear.
- **Exports:** grain. The Aldwater runs through its plains, and irrigation channels spread from the river.
- **History:** Ashmark's tribes raided Halden every winter for generations, when the March's lakes froze.
- **Today:** the Empire buys Halden's grain to pay Ashmark's tribute. The people who suffered the raids now feed the raiders, and Halden remembers.

### The Eastern March — the Border (east)

An Imperial province, owned by the Empire rather than any kingdom. A country of wetlands and lakes where the Aldwater comes down from the mountains.

- **People:** garrisoned and settled from every kingdom.
- **The border:** the wetlands are a natural wall. An army can only cross on the causeways, so keeps guard every causeway and crossing.
- **Winter:** raiding season. When the lakes froze, the wall disappeared.
- **History:** the garrisons failed to hold. Ashmark broke through, and that is how it won the treaty of 748.

### Ashmark — the Beast Kingdom (far east)

The far side of the borderlands. Cold mountains in the west, where the Aldwater rises and the mines are. Badlands in the east, running out toward the hordes.

- **Ruler:** King Fenrick Vargrave, "the Inferno." Every member of House Vargrave is wolfkin.
- **The Ashborn:** Ashmark's free people. Predatory beastkin (wolf, big cat, dog, bear, badger, fox) of Mongolian looks. Proud, and almost always wearing their silks.
- **Slaves:** everyone who is not Ashborn. Mostly prisoners of war, of every people.
- **Culture:** militaristic. Chinese-inspired finery that flows: elegant, yet made to fight in.
- **Exports:** metals and ore, and silk harvested from giant spiders.
- **Weakness:** Ashmark can't feed itself.
- **History:** before Fenrick, separate tribes raided west for food. Fenrick conquered them and united them as the Ashborn. Ashmark is young, built in one man's lifetime.
- **"The Inferno":** in 772, Fenrick's queen was killed in a border clash with the barbarian hordes. He burned the tribes responsible, and the name stuck.

### Beyond Ashmark (farthest east)

The barbarian hordes.

### Maressa — the Coast (west)

The coastal kingdom at the Aldwater's mouth.

- **People:** mostly human, with elves and every kind of beastkin.
- **Culture:** French and artistic. The Empire's home of painters, sculptors, salons, and fashion houses.
- **Trade:** port, navy, and sea trade.

### Zahrun — the Desert (southwest)

Persian in culture.

- **People:** mostly human, with desert beastkin: fennec foxkin, jackalkin, and desert cats.
- **Exports:** precious gems and coveted spices.

### Sylvaren — the Forests (south)

The elven kingdom of the southern forests. Celtic and druidic.

- **People:** mostly elves, with foxkin and rabbitkin.
- **Exports:** exotic pets, enchanted gear, and moon flowers, ground to make magic essence.

### Durnhald — the Highlands (north)

The dwarven kingdom of the northern holds. Scottish highlands, temperate and rainy.

- **People:** mostly dwarves, with wolfkin, bearkin, and boarkin.
- **Exports:** raw timber, ores, ironworks, weapons and armor, and siege weapons and contraptions.

## The Treaty of 748

- Fenrick broke through the Eastern March. Rather than keep losing, the Empire brought Ashmark in as a member kingdom.
- **The price:** the Empire pays Ashmark a tribute in grain, bought from Halden.
- **The silence:** Ashmark keeps slaves, the only kingdom that does, and the Empire looks away.
- **The resentment:** Imperial citizens call the Ashborn uncivilized, but never to their faces. Everyone remembers the raids, Halden most of all.
- **The balance:** the Empire's grain keeps Ashmark at peace, and Ashmark's peace keeps the border safe. Nobody wants to be the one who breaks it.

## Monsters and Dungeons

- Monsters live throughout the kingdoms: kobolds, goblins, dire animals, magical beasts, and the like.
- Ancient ruins, called dungeons, were left by long-forgotten races and lie scattered across the land.

## The Royal Magical College

In Aurelis. It has forged the Empire's magical knights, healers, and scholars for two centuries.

- **Four years:** years 1–2 are theory, years 3–4 are practicals. Fourth years graduate at the end of the year.
- **Paths:** Magical Knight (about 70%), Magical Support (about 20%), Magical Crafting & Research (about 10%).
- **Knight training:** duels, mock battles, monster hunts, dungeon delves, and competitions.
- **Students:** about 1,200, roughly 300 per year, from every kingdom.
- **Who attends:** mostly nobles. Some merchant children with rare talent and the wealth to match. A handful of commoners on scholarship, about seven per year.
- **Rank rules:** officially a meritocracy. In practice, lower-ranked students are expected to obey higher-ranked ones. The College keeps a public battle ranking, and a commoner climbing it upsets everyone above them.

### After the College

Where graduates go:

1. **Imperial Knight Orders** — the Emperor's own knights.
2. **The Eastern March** — the Imperial border garrison.
3. **Kingdom service** — knights in their own king's service.
4. **House retainers** — leading or serving a noble House's forces.
5. **Delvers and adventurers** — free companies that hunt monsters and delve ruins.
